The Mirror Realm

Story Synopsis

In a small town, siblings Aryan and Riya find an antique mirror in their grandparents’ attic. It pulls them into a haunted mirror dimension where everything is twisted. The only way out is by reversing the curse that trapped many before them.

Gameplay Features

  • Use reflections to solve logic puzzles.
  • Swap between characters (Aryan and Riya) to unlock doors.
  • Escape ghostly versions of themselves.

Key Themes

  • GenZ Twist: Sibling dynamic, mirror selfies, TikTok filters gone wrong.
  • Universal Horror: Mirror lore + cursed object theme.
  • Emotional Story Arc: Past trauma, bonding, survival.
